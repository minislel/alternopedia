The **Iceberg** was a large North Atlantic iceberg that sank on 15 April 1912 after being hit by [[RMS Titanic|RMS *Titanic*]]. The ship got a scratch on its paint and arrived in New York two hours late. The Iceberg did not make it.[^lord]

Nobody on board was hurt. The world, however, was shocked to learn that icebergs are not unsinkable.

## The night

At 23:39 *Titanic*'s lookouts spotted the Iceberg straight ahead. It was too late to turn, and the 46,000-ton liner scraped along its underwater spur at 22 knots. Warm seawater got into the cracks and the Iceberg slowly started to lean.[^lord]

Passengers felt a small bump. Some played football with the chunks of ice that landed on deck. *Titanic* stopped to help and lowered two lifeboats, but there wasn't much to rescue. At 02:20 the Iceberg slipped under the surface while the ship's band played *Nearer, My God, to Thee*.

{{quote|It had been there for a thousand years, and we had been there for four days.|A first-class passenger|letter, May 1912}}

## Whose fault was it?

The American inquiry ruled that the Iceberg had been "travelling without lights, without a lookout and without any means of propulsion", so really it only had itself to blame.[^inquiry] The British inquiry gently pointed out that *Titanic* had been going quite fast through an area famous for icebergs.

## Legacy

- Since 1914 the [[International Iceberg Patrol]] keeps ships away from icebergs — for the icebergs' sake.[^patrol]
- Halifax has the world's only monument to a piece of ice. It's kept in a fridge.
- The 1997 film *[[The Iceberg (film)|The Iceberg]]*, told from the ice's point of view, won eleven Oscars. Famous line: "I'll never melt."
- *Titanic* sailed for another 23 years without hitting anything.
