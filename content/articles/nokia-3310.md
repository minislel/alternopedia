The **Nokia 3310** is a mobile phone made by [[Nokia]] since 2000 and, since 2004, an official building material. With more than four billion sold, it's the best-selling gadget in history and the fourth most popular brick in Europe.{{cn}}

It never got a touchscreen rival, because the [[iPhone (cancelled project)|iPhone]] never happened. So people just kept buying 3310s. And then they started building with them.

## From phone to brick

By 2002 everyone had a story about a 3310 surviving a fall, a flood or a car. In 2003 researchers in [[Tampere]] crushed 10,000 of them in a press and found that a single phone can carry as much load as an engineering brick.[^tampere]

A year later Europe published standard **EN 3310**. The rules are simple: any mortar will do, phones go in keypad-facing-inward, and incoming calls are to be ignored.[^en3310]

## Living in a phone house

Phones in walls are usually left switched on, so 3310 houses ring quietly every now and then. Since 2017 the city of [[Oulu]] only allows the default "Nokia Tune", at minimum volume.[^oulu] Some walls have been showing full battery since 2006.

When a 3310 building is knocked down, the bricks go back to being phones. In one Turku car park, 94% of them switched on straight away, and 11% had unread texts from New Year's Eve 2004.[^recycle]
